# Calligraphy Studio fonts and licences

Every font in this folder is licensed under the **SIL Open Font License 1.1** (OFL). Its full licence text is in the matching `OFL-<name>.txt` file. The OFL allows using, embedding (including in exported PNG, JPG and PDF files) and redistributing these fonts with InviteX. It does not allow selling the font files on their own, or shipping a modified font under its reserved name. Source: [Google Fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl).

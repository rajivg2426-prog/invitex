**How to explain InviteX, who to sell it to, and how to launch it on WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook. With ready-to-post messages.**

- **Product:** InviteX, digital invitations with one-tap RSVP
- **Live at:** https://rajivg2426-prog.github.io/invitex/
- **Status of payments:** sharing is **free today**. Paid sharing (a small one-time UPI payment per sharing campaign) is built but **not live yet**. Never advertise a price as live until it is switched on.

## 1. Positioning in one line

> **InviteX makes a beautiful invitation your guests open on WhatsApp and answer with one tap, so you always know who is coming.**

### The short pitch (30 seconds)

Indian families send invitations on WhatsApp, then spend weeks chasing replies by phone. InviteX turns your invitation into a link. Guests open a rich card with the events, venue, directions and photos, and reply with one tap. No app, no sign-up. You see every "yes", "no" and "maybe" in one dashboard, send reminders to people who haven't replied, and check guests in at the gate with a QR code. It works in English, Hindi and Punjabi.

### What InviteX is, and isn't

| InviteX is | InviteX is not |
| --- | --- |
| A designed invitation plus a guest list and RSVP tracker | Just a picture of a card |
| Sent from your own WhatsApp, by you | A bulk-messaging or spam tool |
| For any phone, in the browser | An app guests must download |
| Honest about status: sent, opened, replied | A tool that claims messages were "delivered" |

## 3. Key selling points

Use these in posts, demos and replies. Each line is something the product does today.

1. **Replies in one tap.** Guests tap Yes, Not sure or Can't come. On a personal link their name and number are already filled in.
2. **No app, no sign-up for guests.** It opens straight from WhatsApp on any phone.
3. **Know your numbers.** Confirmed adults and children, event-wise attendance, food preferences and places remaining, live.
4. **Chase less.** See who hasn't opened or replied and send them a reminder.
5. **Designs that look expensive.** A gallery of ready designs with filters and Compare, your own uploaded artwork, an AI design assistant that paints four new designs, and a Calligraphy Studio for handwritten-style cards.
6. **Words written for you.** A writing studio with styles from Traditional and Royal to Poetic, in English, Hindi and Punjabi. Shorter, longer, more emotional or more formal in one tap.
7. **All events in one card.** Mehendi, Haldi, Wedding and Reception, each with Save the date and Get directions.
8. **Print too.** Download the card as PNG, JPG or PDF, or a print-shop file with bleed and crop marks.
9. **Smooth entry on the day.** Every guest gets a QR code. Scan them in at the gate.
10. **Private by design.** Each host sees only their own guests. Guests never see each other's phone numbers.

## 4. Pricing message

### Today

**Design, preview, guest list and messages are always free. Sharing is free right now.** Say: *"Free to make. Free to share during our launch."*

### When paid sharing is switched on (not live yet)

The plan is pay-per-event, not a subscription:

- **One small UPI payment per sharing campaign**, set by the admin between **₹10 and ₹100**, with **₹20** as the default.
- **One payment covers every guest** on the list. Resending, reminders and adding guests in that campaign are free.
- Pay with any UPI app. No card, no subscription, nothing to cancel.

**Draft lines for later** (use only after paid sharing is live and the price is confirmed):

## 5. Launch plan

### Before you post anything (week 0)

- [ ] Make three showcase invitations in the live app: a wedding, a kid's birthday and a puja or housewarming. Use real-looking names, not "test".
- [ ] Take screenshots of each: the cover, the events page, the one-tap reply and the dashboard. Record a 15-second screen video of a guest opening the card and tapping Yes.
- [ ] Set up a WhatsApp Business profile: name **InviteX**, a short description, the app link, a catalogue item "Wedding invitation" with screenshots.
- [ ] Create an Instagram business account and a Facebook Page, both named InviteX, with the same profile picture and the app link in the bio.
- [ ] Decide your launch offer (for example "free during launch").
- [ ] Prepare a short reply for "How much does it cost?" and "Is it safe?" (see section 7).

### WhatsApp launch (week 1)

WhatsApp is where invitations are sent, so it is the most important channel.

1. **Personal network first.** Send the personal message (7.1) to 30 to 50 friends and relatives one by one. Ask each to try it for their next function.
2. **Status.** Post one status a day for 7 days: a design, the one-tap reply, the dashboard, a calligraphy card, the Hindi version, a short video, and a "free during launch" card.
3. **Groups.** Share the group message (7.2) in family, society, alumni and community groups, only where you are a member and it is welcome. One post per group, no repeats.
4. **Broadcast list.** Add people who said "send me the link" to a broadcast list and send updates there, not in groups.
5. **Use the product to market itself.** When you or a friend sends a real invitation through InviteX, every guest sees it. Ask hosts to say "made with InviteX" when guests ask how.

### Instagram launch (weeks 1–4)

Instagram is where couples look for wedding ideas, so lead with design.

1. **Grid start:** post 9 posts before you promote the account, so it looks alive: 3 designs, 2 before/after (paper card vs InviteX), 2 how-it-works carousels, 1 Hindi card, 1 launch announcement.
2. **Reels (2–3 a week):** screen recordings work best. Ideas:
   - "POV: your Bua replies to the wedding invite in one tap"
   - "We stopped calling 300 guests to ask if they're coming"
   - "Watch this invitation open" (the opening animation, with music)
   - "Royal vs Floral vs Calligraphy: which one?" using Compare
   - "Write your wedding invite in Hindi in 10 seconds" (writing studio)
3. **Stories:** polls ("Paper card or digital?"), a question box ("What's the hardest part of inviting guests?"), and a link sticker to the app.
4. **Hashtags (pick 8–12 per post):** #IndianWedding #WeddingInvitation #DigitalInvitation #EInvite #WeddingInvite #ShaadiCard #ShaadiSeason #DesiWedding #BigFatIndianWedding #PunjabiWedding #WeddingPlanning #BirthdayInvitation #GrihaPravesh #InviteX
5. **Collaborate:** offer 5 to 10 small wedding planners, mehndi artists and wedding photographers a free showcase invitation for a client, in return for a tag.

### Facebook launch (weeks 1–4)

Facebook reaches parents, elders and local community groups.

1. Post the launch announcement (7.4) on the InviteX Page, then share it to your own profile.
2. Join local groups (city wedding vendors, NRI community groups, housing societies). Read each group's rules. Post only where promotion is allowed, and prefer helpful posts ("How we tracked 400 RSVPs without one phone call").
3. Post the Hindi version (7.5) for Hindi-speaking groups and the Punjabi line for Punjabi groups.
4. Use Facebook and Instagram together: one post in Meta Business Suite can go to both.
5. **Paid ads (optional, after organic posts work):** start small, for example ₹300 to ₹500 a day for 7 days. Target India, ages 24–45, interests "wedding planning", "engagement", "Indian weddings", plus NRIs in the UK, US, Canada and UAE. Use the best-performing Reel as the ad. Send people straight to the app link.

## 6. Brand voice

- **Warm, simple, respectful.** Write like a helpful family member, not a tech company.
- **Show, don't claim.** A screenshot of the one-tap reply beats "revolutionary RSVP technology".
- **Honest.** Say "sent", "opened" and "replied". Never say messages are "delivered" or "guaranteed".
- **Bilingual.** Post in English and Hindi. Add Punjabi for Punjabi audiences.
- **Colours:** deep maroon and gold, as in the app.
- **Avoid:** fake urgency, fake reviews, claims about payments before they are live, and adding people to groups without asking.
